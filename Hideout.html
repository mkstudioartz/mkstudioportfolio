Animation + Film + Creative Production

Watch the Project

Synopsis

The film features two boys, Ashkii and Zayne, who are searching for a place where they belong after being kicked off the playground by the neighborhood bully. 

Behind the Scenes

About the Project

The Hideout is an 2D animated short film produced in the University of North Carolina at Greensboro for the 2025 Studio Art Animation Capstone Class.

This project was completed using Excel and a Google Notes used to create a Production Timeline to keep track of tasks, and responsibilities and a Production Bible to record the stylistic dscisions and as a hub for all the members to record their palette, brushes, and methods of each of the crew members. For production Toon Boom Storyboard Pro and Harmony was used for both storyboarding and the 2D Animation. Maya was used to create 3D models of important objects and environments to assist with production. For post-production Premiere Pro and After Affects were used to composite the finial animation and all the drafts.

My Role

Role: Project Lead / Animator / Designer

Responsibilities: My responsibilities for this production was as the Producer. I created and kept up with both the Production Timeline and the Production Bible. I animated a few scenes, and was responsible for clean-up, coloring, and compositing. I also worked with the composers as the Music Creative Director, who researched the musical styles, and the musical framework that assigned specific instruments to the individual characters, to communicate personality, relationships, and narrative through sound.

Software: Maya, Adobe Creative Cloud, Toon Boom Harmony, Storyboard Pro, & Milanote.
